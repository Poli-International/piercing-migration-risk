## What is Piercing Migration & Rejection Risk Calculator?

The Piercing Migration & Rejection Risk Calculator is a free educational web tool that assesses the likelihood of a piercing migrating or being rejected by the body. The tool evaluates 7 weighted risk factors: piercing placement, jewelry material, jewelry sizing, aftercare routine, physical trauma history, skin type and healing history, and healing duration.

Each factor carries a different weight in the final calculation. Placement and jewelry material are weighted 3x, making them the most influential factors. Jewelry sizing, aftercare, trauma, and skin type are weighted 2x, while healing duration is weighted 1x.

The calculator produces a weighted risk score that falls into one of four tiers: Low (score 14 or below), Moderate (score 15-22), High (score 23-32), or Very High (score 33+). Each tier includes specific advice and recommendations for next steps.

This tool is designed for educational purposes only and cannot diagnose active migration or infection. It helps users understand which factors contribute most to their piercing's risk profile and what actions they can take to reduce risk.

## Who Should Use This Tool

**Piercing Clients:** Anyone with an existing piercing who wants to assess their current risk level. The tool helps identify controllable risk factors like jewelry material and aftercare habits.

**Professional Piercers:** Studio professionals can use this tool during consultations to demonstrate risk factors to clients and recommend changes. The weighted scoring system provides objective data for client education.

**Studio Staff:** Front desk and aftercare educators can use the tool to reinforce proper aftercare practices and explain why jewelry material and sizing matter for long-term piercing health.

**Prospective Piercing Clients:** Individuals considering a new piercing can use the tool to understand which placements and jewelry choices carry lower risk profiles before getting pierced.

## How to Use the Piercing Migration Risk Calculator

**Step 1: Select Piercing Placement**
Choose from three options: Cartilage/lobe (low surface stress), Navel/nostril (moderate surface stress), or Eyebrow/surface/microdermal (high surface stress). Surface piercings and microdermals carry the highest placement risk.

**Step 2: Choose Jewelry Material**
Select from four categories: BioFlex or ASTM F136 titanium (best), implant-grade 316LVM steel or niobium (good), surgical steel non-implant or acrylic (risky), or unknown alloy/plated/cheap metal (bad). Material quality is weighted 3x in the final score.

**Step 3: Assess Jewelry Sizing**
Indicate whether your jewelry is correctly sized by a professional, slightly short or tight, or clearly too short with embedded risk. Improper sizing increases migration risk.

**Step 4: Evaluate Aftercare Routine**
Choose from saline spray only twice daily, inconsistent or mild soap use, or harsh products like alcohol or rotating jewelry. Proper aftercare significantly reduces risk.

**Step 5: Consider Physical Trauma**
Select from no trauma with well-protected placement, occasional minor snags, or frequent trauma from clothing or sleep. Repeated trauma drives migration.

**Step 6: Account for Skin Type and Healing History**
Choose from heals quickly with no keloid history, slow healer with occasional irritation, or keloid-prone with prior rejection history. Skin type affects healing outcomes.

**Step 7: Specify Healing Duration**
Select from less than 3 months (initial healing), 3-12 months (mid-healing), over 12 months (fully healed), or less than 3 months with warning signs. Early warning signs in fresh piercings carry the highest duration risk.

**Step 8: Calculate Your Score**
Click the "Calculate Risk Score" button. The tool displays your weighted score, risk tier, a visual risk bar, and specific advice for your risk level.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q1: What does the Piercing Migration & Rejection Risk Calculator measure?**
A: The calculator measures 7 weighted risk factors: placement, jewelry material, jewelry sizing, aftercare routine, physical trauma, skin type and healing history, and healing duration. Each factor contributes a weighted score that determines your overall risk tier.

**Q2: Can this tool diagnose active piercing rejection?**
A: No. This tool provides a general educational risk assessment only. It cannot diagnose active migration or infection. If you suspect active migration, consult a professional piercer or healthcare provider.

**Q3: Why are placement and jewelry material weighted 3x?**
A: Placement determines the mechanical stress on the piercing, and material determines biocompatibility. Both factors have the strongest evidence linking them to migration and rejection outcomes, so they receive the highest weight in the scoring system.

**Q4: What is the difference between migration and rejection?**
A: Migration is the gradual movement of a piercing from its original placement. Rejection occurs when the body pushes the jewelry completely out. Migration often precedes rejection and is a warning sign that risk factors need addressing.

**Q5: How accurate is the risk score?**
A: The score is a weighted composite of self-reported factors. Accuracy depends on honest and accurate input. The tool is designed for educational purposes and should not replace professional assessment.

**Q6: What should I do if my score is Very High?**
A: The tool recommends seeing a professional piercer urgently. Do not attempt to remove deeply migrated jewelry yourself. A piercer can assess the situation and recommend jewelry changes, downsizing, or retiring the piercing.

**Q7: Can changing jewelry material reduce my risk?**
A: Yes. The tool identifies jewelry material as the single most controllable risk factor. Switching to BioFlex polymer or ASTM F136 titanium can significantly reduce migration risk, especially for surface and bending placements.

**Q8: Does the tool work for all piercing types?**
A: The tool covers three placement categories: cartilage/lobe, navel/nostril, and eyebrow/surface/microdermal. These categories represent the most common piercing placements with varying surface stress levels.

**Q9: How often should I use this calculator?**
A: Use the calculator whenever your piercing circumstances change, such as after switching jewelry, experiencing trauma, or noticing changes in healing progress. Regular assessment helps catch risk factors early.

**Q10: What warning signs should I watch for regardless of my score?**
A: Watch for skin thinning over the jewelry bar, entry/exit holes moving closer to the surface, persistent redness or raised scar tissue, and jewelry feeling shallower than when first pierced.
