Piercing Migration & Rejection Risk

Evaluate anatomical, jewelry, and mechanical factors that influence migration and rejection. Identify warning signs early, record repeat measurements over time, and know when to see your piercer.

Risk Factor Assessment

Important Clinical Notice: This tool provides educational risk factor analysis and tracking based on established professional practice. It cannot diagnose active infection, rejection, or structural tissue damage. If you observe jewellery sinking into swollen tissue, progressive skin thinning, fever, extreme heat, or severe pain, see a professional body piercer or physician immediately. Never attempt to remove an actively migrating or embedded piercing yourself.
